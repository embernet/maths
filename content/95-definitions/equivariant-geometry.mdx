## Definition

Traditional physics-based approaches — sampling this space by molecular dynamics or Monte Carlo — cannot explore it thoroughly for large proteins. AlphaFold2's approach is different: it learns a **scoring function** from the Protein Data Bank (roughly 200,000 experimentally determined structures), using a transformer neural network with **equivariant geometry**.
