Dividing by $S_n(f)$ is **whitening** — down-weighting frequency bands where the detector is noisier. The quantity $\rho$ (the signal-to-noise ratio) follows a non-central chi-squared distribution when a signal is present; a threshold of $\rho \geq 8$ is standard.
