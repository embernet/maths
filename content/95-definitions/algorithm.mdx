## Definition

An **algorithm** is a finite, deterministic, step-by-step procedure that solves a class of problems. The word derives from *Algoritmi* — the twelfth-century Latin transliteration of al-Khwarizmi's name — via his treatise on arithmetic with Hindu-Arabic numerals.
