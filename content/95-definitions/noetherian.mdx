## Definition

Her *Idealtheorie in Ringbereichen* (1921) and the series of papers that followed developed the abstract theory of **rings** and **ideals**. She generalised [Dedekind](/mathematicians/dedekind)'s theory of ideals from number rings to arbitrary commutative rings, and identified the structural properties — now called **Noetherian** — that made the theory work. A ring is Noetherian if every ascending chain of ideals eventually stabilises. This condition is precisely what ensures that factorisation and ideal theory behave well.
