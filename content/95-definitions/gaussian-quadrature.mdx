## Definition

**Gaussian quadrature** uses $n$ carefully chosen quadrature points to integrate polynomials of degree up to $2n - 1$ exactly. The points and weights are determined by orthogonality conditions, giving much higher accuracy than evenly-spaced rules for smooth functions.
