## Definition

With four satellites you have four equations and four unknowns (including an unknown receiver clock offset). With more satellites — modern receivers use twelve or more — the system is **over-determined**: more equations than unknowns. No exact solution exists (every measurement has noise), so you instead minimise the sum of squared residuals. This is the **method of least squares**, developed by [Gauss](/mathematicians/gauss) and [Legendre](/mathematicians/legendre) at the turn of the nineteenth century.
