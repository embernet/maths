## Definition

**Static analysis** of a structure reduces to solving $K\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{f}$, where $K$ is the **stiffness matrix** (assembled from element stiffness matrices in the finite element method), $\mathbf{u}$ the vector of nodal displacements, and $\mathbf{f}$ the applied forces. This is a large linear system whose solution gives the deformation of the structure under load.
