## Definition

Key properties:
- $K(x) \leq |x| + c$ (you can always describe $x$ by copying it, up to a constant)
- Most strings of length $n$ have $K(x) \approx n$ (they are incompressible — no short description exists)
- $K$ is not computable (computing it would solve the halting problem)
- **Coding theorem:** $K(x) \approx -\log_2 P_{\text{opt}}(x)$ where $P_{\text{opt}}$ is the optimal compressor
