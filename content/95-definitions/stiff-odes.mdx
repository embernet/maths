## Definition

**Stiff ODEs** — those with solutions on widely separated timescales — cause explicit methods to require impractically small step sizes. **Implicit methods** (where the next step is defined by an implicit equation involving $f(t_{n+1}, y_{n+1})$) are stable for large step sizes but require solving a nonlinear system at each step.
