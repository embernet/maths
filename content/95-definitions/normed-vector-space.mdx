## Definition

A **normed vector space** is a vector space $V$ with a norm $\|\cdot\| : V \to [0,\infty)$ satisfying:
1. $\|v\| = 0 \Leftrightarrow v = 0$.
2. $\|\alpha v\| = |\alpha|\|v\|$ for scalars $\alpha$.
3. $\|u + v\| \le \|u\| + \|v\|$ (triangle inequality).
