## Definition

This is the **weak formulation**: find $u \in H^1_0(\Omega)$ such that $a(u,v) = F(v)$ for all $v \in H^1_0(\Omega)$, where $a(u,v) = \int \nabla u \cdot \nabla v\, dx$ and $F(v) = \int fv\, dx$.
