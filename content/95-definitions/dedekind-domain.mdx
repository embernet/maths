## Definition

[Dedekind](/mathematicians/dedekind) restored unique factorisation by replacing elements with **ideals** — special subsets of the ring. In a **Dedekind domain** (an integrally closed Noetherian domain of dimension 1), every nonzero ideal factors uniquely into a product of prime ideals, even when elements themselves don't factor uniquely.
