## Definition

A **graph** $G = (V, E)$ consists of a set of **vertices** $V$ and a set of **edges** $E \subseteq V \times V$. Graphs model anything with pairwise relationships: road networks, social connections, the World Wide Web, molecular structures, dependency graphs in software.
