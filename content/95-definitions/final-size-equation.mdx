## Definition

The **final size equation** relates the fraction $z$ of the population ultimately infected to $R_0$: $1 - z = e^{-R_0 z}$. This implicit equation shows that not everyone gets infected even in a large epidemic — the epidemic "runs out of susceptibles" before everyone is reached.
