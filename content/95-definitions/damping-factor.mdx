## Definition

In practice, the web graph has "dangling nodes" (pages with no outlinks) and disconnected components, which violate irreducibility. The fix is the **damping factor** $d$: with probability $d \approx 0.85$ the surfer follows a link; with probability $1-d$ they jump to a uniformly random page. The modified matrix $\tilde{P} = d P + (1-d) \mathbf{1}\mathbf{v}^T$ (where $\mathbf{v}$ is a personalisation vector) is irreducible by construction, and its stationary distribution is the PageRank vector.
