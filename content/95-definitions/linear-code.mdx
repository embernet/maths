## Definition

A **linear code** $\mathcal{C}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{F}_q^n$ (the vector space of $n$-dimensional vectors over a finite field with $q$ elements). A **$(n, k)$ code** encodes $k$ information symbols into $n$ transmitted symbols; the $(n-k)$ redundant symbols allow error detection and correction.
