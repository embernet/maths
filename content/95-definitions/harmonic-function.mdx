## Definition

These are the **Cauchy-Riemann equations**. They are necessary conditions for complex differentiability (with continuous partial derivatives, also sufficient). They say that the real and imaginary parts of an analytic function are not independent — they are deeply intertwined. Each is a **harmonic function**: $\nabla^2 u = u_{xx} + u_{yy} = 0$ and $\nabla^2 v = 0$. Harmonic functions are solutions of Laplace's equation and appear throughout physics.
