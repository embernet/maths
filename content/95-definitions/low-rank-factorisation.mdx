## Definition

Let $R$ be an $m \times n$ matrix where $R_{ij}$ is user $i$'s rating of item $j$ (missing if user $i$ has not rated item $j$). The **low-rank factorisation** model approximates $R \approx UV^T$, where $U$ is $m \times k$ (a matrix of $k$-dimensional user latent vectors) and $V$ is $n \times k$ (item latent vectors). The rank $k$ (typically 10–200) is far smaller than $m$ or $n$.
