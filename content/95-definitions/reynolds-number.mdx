## Definition

The **Reynolds number** $Re = \rho UL/\mu$ (ratio of inertial to viscous forces) governs the character of flow: $Re \ll 1$ gives Stokes flow (viscosity dominates, no turbulence); $Re \gg 1$ gives inertia-dominated turbulent flow. Blood in capillaries has $Re \approx 0.001$; flow over an aircraft wing has $Re \approx 10^7$.
