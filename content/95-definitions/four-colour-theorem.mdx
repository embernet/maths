## Definition

The **four-colour theorem** (proved by computer in 1976, simplified but still needing a computer in the 1990s): any planar graph can be 4-coloured. Proved by Appel and Haken, it was the first major theorem proved by exhaustive computer case-checking — and controversial for exactly that reason.
