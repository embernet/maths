## Definition

The **maximum likelihood estimate (MLE)** is $\hat{\theta} = \arg\max_\theta L(\theta)$, or equivalently (and numerically more tractable) $\hat{\theta} = \arg\max_\theta \ell(\theta)$ where $\ell = \log L$ is the **log-likelihood**.
