## Definition

He proved this with the **diagonal argument** (1891, though a different proof appeared in 1874). Suppose, for contradiction, that the real numbers in $[0,1]$ can be listed: $r_1, r_2, r_3, \ldots$ Construct a new real number $d$ whose $n$th decimal digit differs from the $n$th decimal digit of $r_n$. Then $d$ is not on the list — it differs from $r_n$ at position $n$ for every $n$. Contradiction. The list cannot be complete.
