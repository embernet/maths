## Definition

Coupled with the **heat equation** for temperature, the continuity equation for mass conservation, and equations of state for density, the full system has no closed-form solution. It must be **discretised**: the globe is divided into a grid of cells (typically 50–100 km horizontally, with 50–100 vertical layers), and the PDEs are replaced by finite-difference or finite-element approximations at each grid point.
