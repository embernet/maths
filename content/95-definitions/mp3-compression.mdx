## Definition

**MP3 compression** uses the **modified discrete cosine transform** (MDCT) to decompose audio into frequency sub-bands, combined with a **psychoacoustic model** that determines which sub-bands are masked by louder nearby frequencies (the human auditory system ignores masked components). Discarding masked coefficients provides the compression, and the remaining ones are encoded with quantisation levels chosen to keep quantisation noise below the masking threshold.
