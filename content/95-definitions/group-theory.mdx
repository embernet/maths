## Definition

**Group theory** enters more generally: the **hidden subgroup problem** asks for a subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ given only black-box access to a function that is constant on cosets of $H$. Shor's algorithm solves this for $G = \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$; the general framework extends to other groups, connecting quantum computing to the representation theory developed in abstract algebra courses.
