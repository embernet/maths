## Definition

A holomorphic function with non-zero derivative is **conformal**: it preserves angles (though not generally lengths). The **Riemann mapping theorem** states that any simply connected proper open subset of $\mathbb{C}$ can be mapped conformally onto the unit disk. This has applications in fluid dynamics (transforming complicated geometries into simple ones) and electrostatics.
