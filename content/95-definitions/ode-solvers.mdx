## Definition

**ODE solvers**: Euler's method $y_{n+1} = y_n + hf(t_n, y_n)$ is simple but only first-order accurate. Runge-Kutta methods (particularly RK4) achieve fourth-order accuracy. Stiff ODEs (with very different timescales) require implicit methods.
