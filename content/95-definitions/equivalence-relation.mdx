## Definition

**Relations** generalise functions. An **equivalence relation** on a set partitions it into classes of "equivalent" elements. The classic example is arithmetic modulo $n$: declare two integers equivalent if they have the same remainder on division by $n$. The resulting equivalence classes $\{[0], [1], \ldots, [n-1]\}$ form the integers modulo $n$, written $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ or $\mathbb{Z}_n$. You can add and multiply these classes, and you get a perfectly consistent arithmetic. This is the foundation of cryptography.
