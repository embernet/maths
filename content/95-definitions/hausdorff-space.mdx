## Definition

A **Hausdorff space** is one where any two distinct points can be separated by disjoint open sets. Metric spaces are always Hausdorff. Many non-metric topological spaces are not Hausdorff, and their properties can be pathological. Most spaces arising in analysis and geometry are Hausdorff.
