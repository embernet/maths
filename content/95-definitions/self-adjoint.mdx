## Definition

The **adjoint** $T^*$ of $T$ satisfies $\langle Tv, w\rangle = \langle v, T^*w\rangle$. An operator is **self-adjoint** (Hermitian) if $T = T^*$. Self-adjoint operators have real eigenvalues (the spectral theorem, stated but perhaps not fully proved at this level) — this is the mathematical foundation of quantum mechanics, where observables are self-adjoint operators.
