## Definition

3. **Factorial design:** Test multiple factors simultaneously, not one at a time. A $2^k$ factorial design with $k$ binary factors requires $2^k$ runs but allows estimation of all main effects and all interaction effects. Testing factors one at a time is inefficient and misses interactions.
