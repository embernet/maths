## Definition

The **complex potential** $w(z) = \phi(x,y) + i\psi(x,y)$ (where $\psi$ is the stream function satisfying $\nabla^2 \psi = 0$ and $\psi = \text{const}$ on streamlines) is an **analytic function** of $z = x + iy$. The Cauchy-Riemann equations ensure that $\phi$ and $\psi$ are harmonic conjugates.
