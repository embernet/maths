## Definition

**Turbulence** remains one of the great unsolved problems of classical physics. Kolmogorov's 1941 theory ($K41$) predicts the **energy spectrum** $E(k) \sim k^{-5/3}$ in the inertial range of turbulence (where energy cascades from large scales to small without dissipation). This is confirmed experimentally but derived from dimensional analysis and scaling arguments rather than from the Navier-Stokes equations directly.
