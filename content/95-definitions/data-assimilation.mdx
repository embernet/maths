## Definition

**Data assimilation** combines the model forecast with millions of observations (from weather stations, radiosondes, satellites, aircraft, and ocean buoys) to produce the best estimate of the current atmospheric state. Mathematically this is a constrained least-squares problem (4D-Var assimilation), minimising the weighted sum of forecast error and observation departure over a time window.
