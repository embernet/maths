## Definition

The **Fisher information** $I(\theta) = -\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2}\log f(X;\theta)\right]$ quantifies how much information a random variable carries about the parameter $\theta$. The **Cramér–Rao bound** states that no unbiased estimator can have variance less than $1/I(\theta)$, making the Fisher information the fundamental limit on estimation precision.
