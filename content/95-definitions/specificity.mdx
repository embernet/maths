## Definition

Define:
- **Sensitivity** (true positive rate): $P(\text{test+} \mid \text{disease+})$
- **Specificity** (true negative rate): $P(\text{test-} \mid \text{disease-}) = 1 - P(\text{test+} \mid \text{disease-})$
- **Prevalence**: $P(\text{disease+})$
