## Definition

This is a much stronger condition than real differentiability because $h$ can approach from any direction in the complex plane. The condition is equivalent to the **Cauchy–Riemann equations**: writing $f = u + iv$, we need $\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}$ and $\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}$.
