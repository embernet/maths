## Definition

This parallel is not a coincidence: both $\mathbb{Z}$ and the polynomial ring $\mathbb{F}[x]$ are examples of **Euclidean domains** — an abstract algebraic structure that appears in core mathematics. Recognising structural parallels is one of the great skills that mathematics teaches.
