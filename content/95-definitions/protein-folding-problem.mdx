## Definition

Every living cell relies on proteins — molecules that fold from a one-dimensional chain of amino acids into precise three-dimensional shapes that determine their function. Predicting the shape from the sequence (the **protein folding problem**) had been an open challenge since the 1970s. DeepMind's AlphaFold2 (2020) solved it at near-experimental accuracy, revolutionising drug discovery and structural biology. The mathematics involves optimisation in vast configuration spaces, geometric deep learning, and attention mechanisms built on linear algebra.
