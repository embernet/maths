## Definition

**Degree** of a vertex $v$: the number of edges incident to $v$. The **handshaking lemma**: $\sum_{v \in V} \deg(v) = 2|E|$ (each edge contributes 2 to the total degree count). Immediate corollary: the number of odd-degree vertices is always even.
