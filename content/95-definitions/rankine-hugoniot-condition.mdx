## Definition

**Shock waves**: when the characteristics of this PDE (curves along which information propagates) converge, the density becomes multi-valued and a discontinuity (shock) forms. This is the traffic jam: a boundary between free flow (low density, high speed) moving forward, and congested flow (high density, low speed) moving backward. The shock speed is given by the **Rankine-Hugoniot condition**: $c_{\text{shock}} = [q]_{\text{jump}} / [\rho]_{\text{jump}}$ — the ratio of jumps in flux and density. For typical traffic conditions, this speed is negative (the jam propagates backward even as vehicles pass through it).
