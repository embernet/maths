## Definition

[Poincaré](/mathematicians/poincare) argued in *La Science et l'Hypothèse* (1902) that the axioms of geometry are not truths about physical space but **conventions** — choices we make for their convenience and utility. If physical experiments showed that Euclidean geometry failed (e.g., the angles of a large triangle do not sum to 180°), we would not abandon Euclidean geometry but instead modify the physics (e.g., attribute the discrepancy to curved light paths).
