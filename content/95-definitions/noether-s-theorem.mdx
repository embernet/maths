## Definition

**Noether's theorem** (1915, published 1918) is one of the deepest results in mathematical physics. It states that every continuous symmetry of the action of a physical system corresponds to a conserved quantity. Time-translation symmetry gives conservation of energy. Spatial-translation symmetry gives conservation of linear momentum. Rotational symmetry gives conservation of angular momentum. The theorem unifies and explains these conservation laws at a fundamental level; it underlies all of modern theoretical physics.
