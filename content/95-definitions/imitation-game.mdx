## Definition

In "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950), [Turing](/mathematicians/turing) proposed replacing the question "Can machines think?" with an operational test he called the **imitation game**. An interrogator communicates by written messages with two parties — one human, one machine — and must decide which is which. If a machine can perform indistinguishably from a human across a wide range of topics, Turing argued, we should attribute thought to it in the same sense we attribute thought to other humans.
