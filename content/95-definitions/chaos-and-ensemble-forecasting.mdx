## Definition

**Chaos and ensemble forecasting**: the atmosphere is a chaotic dynamical system. [Poincaré](/mathematicians/poincare) identified sensitivity to initial conditions in the three-body problem; for the atmosphere, Edward Lorenz quantified it in 1963. Small errors in the initial state grow exponentially, making deterministic forecasts unreliable beyond roughly ten days. The operational response is **ensemble forecasting**: run fifty slightly perturbed versions of the model, and treat the spread of forecasts as a probabilistic distribution of outcomes.
