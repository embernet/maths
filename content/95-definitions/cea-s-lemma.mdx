## Definition

**Céa's lemma** gives the best possible error bound: $\|u - u_h\|_{H^1} \le C \inf_{v_h \in V_h} \|u - v_h\|_{H^1}$. The FEM solution is quasi-optimal: up to the constant $C$, it is as good as the best approximation from $V_h$. Mesh refinement and higher-order elements improve the approximation, with convergence rates controlled by Sobolev embedding theorems.
