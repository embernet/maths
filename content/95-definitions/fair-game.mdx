## Definition

**Expected value**: for a random payoff $X$ with distribution $p$, the expected value $E[X] = \sum_x x\, p(X = x)$. A **fair game** has $E[X] = 0$; a game with $E[X] > 0$ favours the player. Roulette with 37 numbers and a single-zero house slot has $E[\text{payout on a single number}] = 36 \times (1/37) - 1 \times (36/37) = -1/37 \approx -2.7\%$: the house keeps 2.7 pence of every pound wagered on average.
