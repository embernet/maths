## Definition

- **Injective** (one-to-one): $f(a_1) = f(a_2) \Rightarrow a_1 = a_2$. Distinct inputs give distinct outputs.
- **Surjective** (onto): every element of $B$ is the image of at least one element of $A$.
- **Bijective**: both injective and surjective. A bijection between $A$ and $B$ establishes a perfect pairing — every element of $A$ corresponds to exactly one element of $B$, and vice versa.
