## Definition

**Voronoi diagrams**: given a set of base station locations $\{p_1, \ldots, p_n\}$, the **Voronoi cell** of $p_i$ is the set of points in the plane closer to $p_i$ than to any other base station. These cells tile the plane exactly and define natural service areas. Voronoi diagrams arise from the geometry of nearest-neighbour assignment and are dual to the **Delaunay triangulation** — a triangulation that maximises the minimum angle of any triangle, favoured by finite element mesh generators.
