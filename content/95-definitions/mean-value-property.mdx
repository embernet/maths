## Definition

Solutions to $\nabla^2 u = 0$ (harmonic functions) satisfy the **mean value property**: the value of $u$ at any point equals the average of $u$ over any sphere (or circle) centred at that point. This implies the **maximum principle**: a harmonic function on a bounded domain cannot have an interior maximum or minimum — these must occur on the boundary.
