## Definition

The **expectation** of a random variable $X$ is $E[X] = \sum_k k\, P(X=k)$ (discrete) or $\int_{-\infty}^\infty x\, f(x)\, dx$ (continuous). Expectation is linear: $E[aX + bY] = aE[X] + bE[Y]$.
