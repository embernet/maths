## Definition

[Euler's](/mathematicians/euler) famous analysis of the Königsberg bridge problem (1736) founded graph theory. Seven bridges connect four landmasses; can you cross each bridge exactly once and return to your starting point? Euler's answer: no, because the four landmasses have odd degree. An **Eulerian circuit** exists if and only if the graph is connected and every vertex has even degree.
