## Definition

[Pythagoras](/mathematicians/pythagoras) (or the Pythagorean school) observed that pleasing musical intervals correspond to simple ratios of string lengths:
- **Octave:** 2:1 (double the length, halve the frequency → octave lower)
- **Perfect fifth:** 3:2
- **Perfect fourth:** 4:3
- **Major third:** 5:4
