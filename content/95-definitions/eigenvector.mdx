## Definition

A vector $\mathbf{v} \ne 0$ is an **eigenvector** of a matrix $A$ with **eigenvalue** $\lambda$ if $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$. The matrix does not rotate $\mathbf{v}$; it only stretches (or compresses, or reverses) it.
