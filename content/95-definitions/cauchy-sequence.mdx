## Definition

A **Cauchy sequence** satisfies $d(x_m, x_n) \to 0$ as $m, n \to \infty$. A metric space is **complete** if every Cauchy sequence converges. Completeness is the key property: $\mathbb{R}$ is complete; $\mathbb{Q}$ is not. The function space $C([0,1])$ with the sup metric is complete — this is the basis for the Banach fixed-point theorem.
