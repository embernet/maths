## Definition

**Relativistic corrections**: without correction, clocks on GPS satellites run faster by about 38 microseconds per day (7 microseconds from special relativity due to satellite velocity, offset by 45 microseconds from general relativity due to weaker gravity at altitude). A 38-microsecond error translates to a position error of over 11 km per day. General-relativistic corrections are therefore baked into the satellite clock rates — one of the few places where general relativity appears as an engineering necessity rather than a curiosity.
