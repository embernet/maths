## Definition

The **Pascaline** (1642) is a mechanical adding machine: a series of interlocking gears where carrying is propagated automatically. When a gear completes a full revolution (representing 9 → 10), it advances the next gear by one tooth. This mimics the carry mechanism of manual addition.
