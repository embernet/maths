## Definition

You already know what it means for a sequence of functions $(f_n)$ to **converge pointwise** to $f$: for each fixed $x$, $f_n(x) \to f(x)$. The trouble is that pointwise convergence is too weak: a sequence of continuous functions can converge pointwise to a discontinuous limit; a sequence of differentiable functions can converge to a non-differentiable limit; the integral $\int_a^b f_n(x)\, dx$ need not converge to $\int_a^b f(x)\, dx$.
