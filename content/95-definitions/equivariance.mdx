## Definition

**Equivariance** means the network's output respects rotations and translations of the input structure: if you rotate the protein coordinates, the predicted structure rotates accordingly. This is a statement about the representation theory of the rotation group $\text{SO}(3)$ — related to the group theory in [Abstract Algebra](/02-core-mathematics/01-abstract-algebra).
