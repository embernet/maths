## Definition

A holomorphic function is automatically infinitely differentiable, equal to its Taylor series, and determined by its values on any open set. The **Cauchy integral theorem** and **Cauchy integral formula** then give: $f(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \oint_C \frac{f(w)}{w - z}\,dw$, so function values inside a contour are determined by boundary values alone.
