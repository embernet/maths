## Definition

The proof constructs the solution via the **Picard iteration**: $y_0(t) = y_0$; $y_{n+1}(t) = y_0 + \int_{t_0}^t f(s, y_n(s))\, ds$. This is a contraction mapping on the space of continuous functions with the sup norm, and the Banach fixed-point theorem gives convergence to the unique solution.
