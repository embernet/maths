## Definition

Banach was a central figure in the Lwów mathematical community that gathered at the Scottish Café from around 1935 onwards. The mathematicians there kept a large notebook — the **Scottish Book** — in which they wrote open problems and offered prizes for their solution (a live goose was offered for one). The Scottish Book survived the war and problems from it continue to drive research in functional analysis.
