## Definition

The **Banach fixed-point theorem**: if $T : X \to X$ is a **contraction** ($\|Tx - Ty\| \le k\|x - y\|$ for some $k < 1$) on a complete metric space $X$, then $T$ has a unique fixed point, and the iteration $x_{n+1} = Tx_n$ converges to it from any starting point.
