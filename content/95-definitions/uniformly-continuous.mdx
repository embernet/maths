## Definition

A function $f$ is **uniformly continuous** on $[a, b]$ if the $\delta$ can be chosen independently of $a$: for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists $\delta > 0$ such that $|x - y| < \delta$ implies $|f(x) - f(y)| < \varepsilon$ for all $x, y$.
