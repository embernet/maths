## Definition

The matrix $P = (p_{ij})$ is the **transition matrix**. Markov chains model queues, random walks, Google's PageRank, and the Metropolis algorithm in Bayesian computation. The key long-run question: does the chain have a **stationary distribution** $\pi = \pi P$, and does it converge to $\pi$ regardless of the starting state? For irreducible aperiodic chains on finite state spaces: yes.
