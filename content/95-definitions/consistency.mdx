## Definition

- **Unbiasedness**: $E[\hat{\theta}] = \theta$. The estimator is "right on average."
- **Consistency**: $\hat{\theta} \to \theta$ in probability as $n \to \infty$.
- **Efficiency**: the variance of $\hat{\theta}$ is as small as possible.
