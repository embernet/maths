## Definition

For compressible flow at higher speeds, the governing equations become nonlinear PDEs (the Euler equations), requiring numerical methods. Modern **computational fluid dynamics** (CFD) discretises the full Navier-Stokes equations on three-dimensional meshes using finite volume methods.
