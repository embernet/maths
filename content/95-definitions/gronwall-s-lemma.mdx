## Definition

**Gronwall's lemma**: if $u(t) \le \alpha(t) + \int_{t_0}^t \beta(s) u(s)\, ds$, then $u(t) \le \alpha(t) + \int_{t_0}^t \alpha(s)\beta(s) e^{\int_s^t \beta(r)dr}\, ds$. This fundamental inequality bounds the "growth of error" and is used to prove continuous dependence on initial conditions.
