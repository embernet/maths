## Definition

**Key properties:**
- Each entry is the sum of the two entries above: $\binom{n}{k} = \binom{n-1}{k-1} + \binom{n-1}{k}$ (Pascal's identity)
- Row $n$ sums to $2^n$
- Diagonal sums give Fibonacci numbers
- The entries are exactly the binomial coefficients in $(x+y)^n$
