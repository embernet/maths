## Definition

**Computing PageRank** at web scale ($\sim 10^{10}$ pages) uses the **power iteration method**: start from any distribution $\pi^{(0)}$, repeatedly compute $\pi^{(k+1)} = \pi^{(k)} \tilde{P}$. Convergence is guaranteed and typically fast when $d < 1$ bounds the second eigenvalue away from 1.
