## Definition

The **principal part** consists of terms with $n < 0$. A singularity is:
- **Removable** if all $c_n = 0$ for $n < 0$ (the singularity can be "filled in").
- A **pole of order $m$** if $c_{-m} \ne 0$ but $c_n = 0$ for all $n < -m$.
- An **essential singularity** if infinitely many $c_n \ne 0$ for $n < 0$.
