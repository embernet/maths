## Definition

The formula transforms complex arithmetic. Multiplying $re^{i\theta} \cdot se^{i\phi} = rse^{i(\theta+\phi)}$ shows that multiplication in $\mathbb{C}$ simultaneously scales by moduli and adds arguments. **De Moivre's theorem** — $(cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^n = \cos(n\theta) + i\sin(n\theta)$ — follows immediately and gives you the $n$th roots of any complex number.
