## Definition

**Kelly criterion**: given a positive-expected-value bet with win probability $p$ and odds $b:1$, the fraction $f^* = (bp - (1-p))/b$ of bankroll to wager maximises the long-run growth rate of wealth. This is the logarithmic utility result of information theory applied to gambling, and it underlies professional sports betting and quantitative hedge fund position sizing.
