## Definition

Geophysicists map underground rock formations — oil reservoirs, aquifers, fault zones — by generating controlled seismic waves (explosions, vibrating trucks, or airguns at sea) and measuring how they reflect and refract off subsurface interfaces. Recovering the velocity and density structure of the earth from measurements at the surface is an **inverse problem**: given the output of the wave equation (the recorded seismograms), infer the coefficients (the earth's properties). The mathematics involves PDEs, Fourier analysis, and computational inverse problem theory.
