## Definition

Voyager 1, at roughly 23 billion kilometres from Earth, communicates at 160 bits per second with a signal so weak that noise dominates. NASA uses **Golay codes** and **convolutional codes** — algebraically structured codes with provable error-correction properties — to recover the original data. Without error-correcting codes, the images from the outer planets could not have been transmitted at all.
