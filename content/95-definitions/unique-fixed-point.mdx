## Definition

Then:
1. $T$ has a **unique fixed point** $x^* \in X$ with $T(x^*) = x^*$.
2. For any starting point $x_0$, the iteration $x_{n+1} = T(x_n)$ converges to $x^*$.
3. The error bound: $d(x_n, x^*) \leq \frac{k^n}{1-k}\, d(x_1, x_0)$.
