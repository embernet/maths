## Definition

The **stationary distribution** $\pi$ of this chain satisfies $\pi P = \pi$ (row vector form) — or equivalently, $\pi$ is a **left eigenvector** of $P$ with eigenvalue $1$. For an irreducible aperiodic chain, the Perron-Frobenius theorem guarantees a unique such vector with non-negative entries summing to 1. The components of $\pi$ are the PageRank scores.
