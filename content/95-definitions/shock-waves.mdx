## Definition

Phantom traffic jams — jams that form on open roads with no accident or obstruction — are one of the more counterintuitive phenomena of everyday life. They arise because traffic flow, like compressible gas dynamics, supports **shock waves**: discontinuities in density that propagate backward through a flow even when vehicles move forward. The mathematics that explains this is the theory of hyperbolic conservation laws, developed in the same framework as fluid dynamics.
