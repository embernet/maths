## Definition

The governing equations are the **primitive equations** — a simplified form of the Navier-Stokes equations appropriate for large-scale atmospheric dynamics, combined with the thermodynamic energy equation, the continuity equation, and the ideal gas law. These are coupled, nonlinear PDEs in four dimensions (three spatial plus time).
