## Definition

[Alan Turing](/mathematicians/turing) (1912–1954) attacked Hilbert's *Entscheidungsproblem* (decision problem): is there an algorithm that can decide whether any given mathematical statement is provable? Turing formalised the notion of "algorithm" through the **Turing machine** — an abstract model of computation — and proved that the halting problem (determining whether a Turing machine will eventually halt) is undecidable. No algorithm can solve it.
