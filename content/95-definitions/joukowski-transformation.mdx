## Definition

The **Joukowski transformation** $z \mapsto \zeta + c^2/\zeta$ maps the circle $|z| = R$ to an airfoil shape. Crucially, because $w$ is analytic, the complex potential of the transformed flow is obtained by substituting — and the lift can be computed from the **Kutta-Joukowski theorem**: $L = \rho U \Gamma$ per unit span, where $\Gamma$ is the circulation. Lift comes entirely from circulation, and circulation is generated by the sharp trailing edge of the airfoil (the Kutta condition).
