## Definition

The first equation is Newton's second law for a fluid element: the left side is mass times acceleration (convective term $(\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{u}$ included); the right side is pressure gradient, viscosity, and body forces. The $\mu\nabla^2\mathbf{u}$ term describes viscous (friction) forces; setting $\mu = 0$ gives the **Euler equations** for inviscid flow.
