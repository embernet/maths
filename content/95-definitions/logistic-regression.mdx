## Definition

**Logistic regression** relaxes the independence assumption by directly modelling $P(C = 1 \mid \mathbf{x}) = \sigma(\boldsymbol{\theta}^T \mathbf{x})$ (where $\sigma$ is the sigmoid function) and estimating $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ by maximum likelihood. Modern spam filters use much richer features and neural network classifiers, but naïve Bayes remains competitive and interpretable.
