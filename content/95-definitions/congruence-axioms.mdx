## Definition

Hilbert's *Grundlagen der Geometrie* (Foundations of Geometry, 1899) replaced Euclid's informal axioms with a rigorous modern axiom system. His axioms are grouped into five families:
1. **Incidence axioms** (e.g., two distinct points determine a unique line)
2. **Order axioms** (betweenness — what it means for a point to be between two others)
3. **Congruence axioms** (equality of lengths and angles)
4. **Parallel axiom** (the Playfair form of the parallel postulate)
5. **Continuity axioms** (Archimedean and completeness axioms)
