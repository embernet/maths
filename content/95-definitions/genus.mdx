## Definition

An algebraic curve (one-dimensional variety) over $\mathbb{C}$ is, as a topological space, a compact Riemann surface — a torus with $g$ handles for some **genus** $g \ge 0$. A sphere is $g=0$; a torus is $g=1$; a pretzel shape is $g=2$.
