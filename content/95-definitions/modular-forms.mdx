## Definition

**Modular forms** (a special case): functions on $\mathbb{H}$ satisfying $f\!\left(\frac{az+b}{cz+d}\right) = (cz+d)^k f(z)$ for $\Gamma = \text{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$. These are central in modern number theory.
