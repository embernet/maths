## Definition

- **Permutations**: ordered arrangements of $r$ from $n$ objects: $P(n,r) = n!/(n-r)!$.
- **Combinations**: unordered selections of $r$ from $n$: $\binom{n}{r} = n!/(r!(n-r)!)$.
- **Multinomial coefficient**: $\binom{n}{k_1, k_2, \ldots, k_m} = n!/(k_1! k_2! \cdots k_m!)$ counts ways to partition $n$ objects into groups of sizes $k_1, k_2, \ldots, k_m$.
- **Inclusion-exclusion**: $|A_1 \cup A_2 \cup \cdots \cup A_n|$ computed by alternating sums of intersections.
