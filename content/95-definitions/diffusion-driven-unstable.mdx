## Definition

The key insight: if the inhibitor diffuses much faster than the activator ($D_v \gg D_u$), a uniform steady state that is stable without diffusion can become **diffusion-driven unstable** — small perturbations grow and settle into spatially periodic patterns. The characteristic wavelength of the resulting pattern is determined by the ratio $D_v/D_u$ and the reaction kinetics. This mechanism is now called a **Turing instability**.
