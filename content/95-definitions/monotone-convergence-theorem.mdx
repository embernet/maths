## Definition

**Monotone Convergence Theorem.** Every bounded monotone sequence converges. (A sequence is *monotone increasing* if $a_{n+1} \ge a_n$ for all $n$.) This is used to define $e = \lim_{n \to \infty}(1 + 1/n)^n$.
