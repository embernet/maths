## Definition

The weak formulation is also the starting point for numerical computation. Restrict to a finite-dimensional subspace $V_h \subset H^1_0$ (piecewise polynomial functions on a mesh) and solve $a(u_h, v_h) = F(v_h)$ for all $v_h \in V_h$. This is the **Galerkin approximation**, the mathematical core of the finite element method.
