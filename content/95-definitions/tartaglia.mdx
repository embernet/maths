## Definition

Niccolò Fontana, known as **Tartaglia** (c.1500–1557), found a method to solve certain cubic equations. He shared his technique in confidence with [Gerolamo Cardano](/mathematicians/cardano), who published it in his *Ars Magna* (1545) — the most important algebra book of the sixteenth century — with attribution to Tartaglia but in a more general form. Tartaglia was furious; Cardano maintained the attribution was fair. The ensuing dispute was bitter and public.
