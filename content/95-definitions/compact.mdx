## Definition

A metric space $K$ is **compact** if every open cover has a finite sub-cover (the Heine-Borel-Lebesgue definition). In $\mathbb{R}^n$, this is equivalent (by the **Heine-Borel theorem**) to being closed and bounded. In general metric spaces, this equivalence fails: you need the notion of **sequentially compact** (every sequence has a convergent subsequence).
