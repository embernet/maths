## Definition

In 1994, [Andrew Wiles](/mathematicians/hardy) proved Fermat's Last Theorem — a problem open for 358 years — by proving a special case of the **Taniyama-Shimura conjecture**: every elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ is modular. This was part of the Langlands programme: a vast web of conjectured connections between automorphic forms, Galois representations, and L-functions that unifies number theory, representation theory, and algebraic geometry.
