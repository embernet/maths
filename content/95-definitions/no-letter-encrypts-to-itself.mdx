## Definition

1. **No letter encrypts to itself** (a constraint of the reflector wiring)
2. **Cribs** — known or guessed plaintext fragments (e.g., standard message openings) — could be tested by seeking self-consistent assignments
3. The **Bombe**, an electromechanical device Turing designed (building on Polish work by Marian Rejewski), ran through rotor positions testing candidate settings in parallel; a contradiction (a letter encrypting to itself) eliminated a setting
