## Definition

The Lebesgue integral is built in stages:
1. **Indicator functions**: $\int \mathbf{1}_A\, d\mu = \mu(A)$.
2. **Simple functions** (finite linear combinations of indicators): $\int \sum a_i \mathbf{1}_{A_i}\, d\mu = \sum a_i \mu(A_i)$.
3. **Non-negative measurable functions**: $\int f\, d\mu = \sup\{\int s\, d\mu : 0 \le s \le f,\ s \text{ simple}\}$.
4. **General functions**: split into $f = f^+ - f^-$ and integrate positive and negative parts separately.
