## Definition

**Hamiltonian paths** — visiting every vertex exactly once — are completely different: there is no known polynomial-time algorithm to determine whether a Hamiltonian path exists (this is an NP-complete problem). The contrast between the easy Euler problem and the hard Hamilton problem is one of the first hints of computational complexity theory.
