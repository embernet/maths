## Definition

[Ramanujan](/mathematicians/ramanujan) proved that highly composite numbers have a specific form: $2^{a_1} 3^{a_2} 5^{a_3} \cdots p_k^{a_k}$ where $a_1 \geq a_2 \geq \cdots \geq a_k \geq 1$ (a product of consecutive primes with non-increasing exponents). He also introduced **superior highly composite numbers** (a stronger variant) and related them to the Riemann hypothesis.
