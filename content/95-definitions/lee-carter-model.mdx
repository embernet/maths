## Definition

**Mortality modelling**: modern actuarial practice fits parametric models to observed mortality data. The **Lee-Carter model** decomposes log-mortality $\ln \mu_{x,t} = \alpha_x + \beta_x \kappa_t + \varepsilon_{x,t}$ where $\kappa_t$ is a mortality index whose future values follow an ARIMA time-series model. This is essentially principal component analysis on a mortality matrix, combined with time-series forecasting — all within the framework of [maximum likelihood estimation](/02-core-year-two/05-probability-and-statistical-inference).
