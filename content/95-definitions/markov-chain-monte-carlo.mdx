## Definition

When conjugacy is unavailable, inference requires numerical approximation: **Markov chain Monte Carlo** (MCMC) samples from the posterior by constructing a Markov chain with stationary distribution $p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D})$; **variational inference** approximates the posterior by a simpler distribution found by minimising the KL divergence.
