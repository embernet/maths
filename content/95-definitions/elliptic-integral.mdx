## Definition

An **elliptic integral** is one of the form $\int R(x, \sqrt{P(x)})\,dx$ where $P$ is a polynomial of degree 3 or 4 with no repeated roots. These cannot generally be expressed in terms of elementary functions — a discovery that motivated an entirely new theory.
