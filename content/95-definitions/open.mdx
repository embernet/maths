## Definition

An **open ball** $B(x, r) = \{y \in X : d(x,y) < r\}$ generalises the interval $(x-r, x+r)$ in $\mathbb{R}$. A set $U \subseteq X$ is **open** if every point of $U$ is the centre of some open ball contained in $U$.
