## Definition

A sufficiently large quantum computer running Shor's algorithm could factorise any 2048-bit integer in hours, breaking all current RSA-based internet security. This threat has triggered a global transition to **post-quantum cryptography**. The mathematics behind Shor's algorithm is the quantum Fourier transform over cyclic groups, combined with the order-finding problem from elementary number theory — pure undergraduate mathematics translated into quantum mechanics.
