## Definition

**Year Two** goes deeper into each strand and introduces new ones. Abstract algebra takes the concrete groups you met in Year One and reveals the general theory. Real analysis becomes metric spaces and rigorous integration. Complex analysis — functions of a complex variable — is one of the most beautiful parts of the entire degree. You will also encounter partial differential equations, statistical inference, and your first taste of numerical computing.
