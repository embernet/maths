## Definition

**Core mathematics** goes deeper into each strand and introduces new ones. Abstract algebra takes concrete groups and reveals the general theory. Real analysis becomes metric spaces and rigorous integration. Complex analysis — functions of a complex variable — is one of the most beautiful parts of the subject. This level also brings partial differential equations, statistical inference, and a first taste of numerical computing.
