## Definition

**Differential geometry and shading**: realistic lighting requires computing surface normals and curvatures. A smooth surface in 3D is described by a parametrisation $\mathbf{r}(u,v)$; the **first fundamental form** $(E\, du^2 + 2F\, du\, dv + G\, dv^2)$ encodes distances, and the **second fundamental form** encodes curvatures. Normal maps encode perturbations to the normal vector for high-frequency surface detail without increasing polygon count.
