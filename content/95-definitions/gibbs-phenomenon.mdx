## Definition

Every digital signal compression scheme — [JPEG, MP3 and friends](/applications/signal-processing-jpeg-mp3) — uses truncated Fourier-style expansions. Knowing when convergence is guaranteed (and where it fails — overshoot at jump discontinuities is called the **Gibbs phenomenon**) is essential for understanding the artefacts you see at sharp edges in compressed images.
