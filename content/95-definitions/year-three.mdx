## Definition

**Specialist areas** are where the subject fans out. You might go deep into algebra (Galois theory, representation theory), analysis (functional analysis, measure theory, topology), applied mathematics (fluid dynamics, mathematical biology, mathematical finance), or statistics and data science. The options represent the full breadth of modern mathematics.
