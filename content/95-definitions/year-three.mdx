## Definition

**Year Three** is where you choose your path. Typically twenty-seven or more optional units are available. You might go deep into algebra (Galois theory, representation theory), into analysis (functional analysis, measure theory, topology), into applied mathematics (fluid dynamics, mathematical biology, mathematical finance), or into statistics and data science. The shape of your degree in Year Three reflects who you are as a mathematician.
