## Definition

The **Borel $\sigma$-algebra** on $\mathbb{R}$ is the smallest $\sigma$-algebra containing all open intervals. The **Lebesgue measure** $\lambda$ on $\mathbb{R}$ is the unique measure with $\lambda([a,b]) = b - a$ — it assigns to every interval its length. The Lebesgue measure of the rationals $\lambda(\mathbb{Q}) = 0$ (countable union of measure-zero singletons).
