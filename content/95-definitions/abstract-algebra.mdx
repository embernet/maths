## Definition

**Abstract Algebra:** Dummit and Foote's *Abstract Algebra* is the standard graduate-level reference; Michael Artin's *Algebra* is more accessible and has a geometric flavour. Herstein's *Topics in Algebra* is the classic introductory text.
