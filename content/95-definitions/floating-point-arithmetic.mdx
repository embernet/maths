## Definition

**Floating-point arithmetic**: computers represent real numbers with finite precision. Rounding errors accumulate; some algorithms are stable (errors stay small) and others unstable (errors grow catastrophically). The condition number $\kappa(A)$ of a matrix measures how sensitive the solution of $Ax = b$ is to perturbations in $b$.
