## Definition

**Numerical integration**: the trapezoid rule $\int_a^b f\, dx \approx h\sum f(x_i)$ has error $O(h^2)$; Simpson's rule is $O(h^4)$. Gaussian quadrature achieves exact integration of polynomials up to degree $2n-1$ with $n$ function evaluations.
