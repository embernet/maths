## Definition

[Fisher's](/mathematicians/fisher) maximum likelihood principle: choose $\hat{\theta}$ to maximise the **likelihood** $L(\theta) = \prod_{i=1}^n f(x_i; \theta)$, or equivalently the **log-likelihood** $\ell(\theta) = \sum_{i=1}^n \log f(x_i; \theta)$.
