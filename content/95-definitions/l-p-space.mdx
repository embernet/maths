## Definition

For $1 \le p < \infty$, the **$L^p$ space** consists of measurable functions with $\int |f|^p\, d\mu < \infty$, equipped with the norm $\|f\|_p = (\int |f|^p)^{1/p}$. These are Banach spaces. $L^2$ is a Hilbert space and the natural setting for Fourier analysis.
