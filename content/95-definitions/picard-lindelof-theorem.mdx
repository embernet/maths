## Definition

The **Picard-Lindelöf theorem**: given the ODE $y' = f(t, y)$ with initial condition $y(t_0) = y_0$, if $f$ is continuous and Lipschitz in $y$ (meaning $|f(t,y_1) - f(t,y_2)| \le L|y_1 - y_2|$ for some constant $L$), then there exists a unique local solution.
