## Definition

For very large, sparse matrices (millions of rows), storing a full dense matrix is impractical. **Krylov subspace methods** exploit sparsity by working with the sequence of vectors $\{b, Ab, A^2b, \ldots, A^{k-1}b\}$ (the Krylov subspace) rather than the full matrix.
