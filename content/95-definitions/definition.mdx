## Definition

His *Cours d'Analyse* (1821) gave the first rigorous treatment of limits: rather than appealing to "infinitely small quantities," he defined the limit of a function in terms of inequalities. The **ε–δ definition** — that $f(x) \to L$ as $x \to a$ means that for every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists $\delta > 0$ such that $|x - a| < \delta$ implies $|f(x) - L| < \varepsilon$ — is Cauchy's legacy, and it is the definition every analysis student encounters in Year One or Year Two.
