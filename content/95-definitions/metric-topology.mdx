## Definition

Examples:
- **Metric topology**: open balls generate the topology of any metric space.
- **Discrete topology**: $\tau = 2^X$ (every subset is open).
- **Indiscrete topology**: $\tau = \{\emptyset, X\}$ (only the trivial open sets).
- **Zariski topology**: used in algebraic geometry, where closed sets are algebraic varieties.
