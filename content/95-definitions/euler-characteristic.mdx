## Definition

He also developed **homology** — sequences of abelian groups $H_n(X)$ associated to a topological space, measuring $n$-dimensional holes. The **Euler characteristic** $\chi = V - E + F$ for polyhedra is $\sum_n (-1)^n \text{rank}(H_n)$.
