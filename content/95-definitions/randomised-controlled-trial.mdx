## Definition

A **randomised controlled trial** (RCT) randomly assigns participants to a treatment group or a control (placebo) group. The outcome of interest is measured for each participant, and the question is whether the difference in outcomes between groups is large enough to be unlikely under the null hypothesis of no treatment effect.
