## Definition

The **harmonic series** — the sequence $f, 2f, 3f, 4f, \ldots$ of integer multiples of a fundamental frequency — is the set of pure tones a vibrating string produces simultaneously. The consonant intervals are those whose frequency ratios are ratios of small integers.
