## Definition

Cantor then did something more extraordinary: he proved that infinities have different sizes. The natural numbers and the rational numbers are both **countably infinite** (there is a bijection between them). But the real numbers are **uncountably infinite**: no bijection between $\mathbb{N}$ and $\mathbb{R}$ exists. The proof — Cantor's diagonal argument — is one of the most beautiful in mathematics.
