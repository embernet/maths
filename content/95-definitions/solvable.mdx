## Definition

A group $G$ is **solvable** if it has a composition series in which every factor is abelian. The symmetric groups $S_n$ are solvable for $n \leq 4$ but not for $n \geq 5$, because $A_5$ (the alternating group on 5 elements) is simple and non-abelian.
