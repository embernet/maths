## Definition

Key property: **Parseval's theorem** $\int |f|^2 = \int |\hat{f}|^2$ — the total energy is preserved. The **convolution theorem**: $\widehat{f * g} = \hat{f} \cdot \hat{g}$ — convolution in time becomes multiplication in frequency.
