## Definition

**Stochastic extensions** model small populations (relevant at the onset or near extinction), using branching processes and continuous-time Markov chains. **Network models** replace the mean-field assumption (homogeneous mixing) with explicit contact graphs, coupling the epidemic dynamics with the graph-colouring and spectral theory of [discrete mathematics](/02-core-mathematics/06-programming-and-discrete-mathematics).
