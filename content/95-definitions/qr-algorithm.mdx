## Definition

The **QR algorithm** iterates: write $A = Q_k R_k$ (QR factorisation), then set $A_{k+1} = R_k Q_k$. This sequence converges (under mild conditions) to an upper triangular matrix (Schur form) whose diagonal entries are the eigenvalues. The QR algorithm is the workhorse of numerical eigenvalue computation and underlies MATLAB's  function and most scientific computing eigensolvers.
