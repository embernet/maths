## Definition

The **Gaussian distribution** — the bell curve — $\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}$ bears his name because he used it to model astronomical measurement errors. He also developed the method of **least squares** (independently of [Legendre](/mathematicians/legendre), and before), which is the foundation of linear regression: find the line that minimises the sum of squared deviations from the data. The method was crucial for the rediscovery of the asteroid Ceres after it was lost behind the sun — Gauss predicted where it would reappear, and he was right.
