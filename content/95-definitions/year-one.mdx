## Definition

**Year One** builds the foundations. You will meet four broad areas: algebra and linear algebra; real analysis (the rigorous treatment of limits and continuity); calculus and its methods (extending to multiple variables, vectors, and differential equations); and probability and statistics. These are not independent subjects — they constantly refer to each other. Year One is also where you learn proof: how to construct a logical argument, how to read a definition, and why the phrase "it seems obvious" is not acceptable in mathematics.
