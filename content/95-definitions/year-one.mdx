## Definition

**Foundations** is the entry level: the four pillars — algebra and linear algebra; real analysis (the rigorous treatment of limits and continuity); calculus and its methods (extending to several variables, vectors, and differential equations); and probability and statistics. These are not independent subjects; they constantly refer to one another. Foundations is also where proof is introduced: how to construct a logical argument, how to read a definition, and why "it seems obvious" is not acceptable in mathematics.
