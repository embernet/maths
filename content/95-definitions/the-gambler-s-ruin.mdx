## Definition

**The gambler's ruin**: a player starting with $k$ units, playing against a house with $(N - k)$ units, making fair bets, has probability $k/N$ of winning all $N$ units before going broke. For an unfair game, the probability of ruin approaches 1 exponentially fast as $k/N \to 0$ — a result proved using difference equations for the probability generating function, a technique related to the generating functions in [discrete mathematics](/02-core-year-two/06-programming-and-discrete-mathematics).
