## Definition

To find eigenvalues, solve the **characteristic equation**:
$$\det(A - \lambda I) = 0.$$
This gives a polynomial in $\lambda$ — the characteristic polynomial — whose roots are the eigenvalues. The eigenvectors for each eigenvalue form the corresponding **eigenspace** (the kernel of $A - \lambda I$).
