## Definition

A **measure** $\mu : \mathcal{F} \to [0, \infty]$ satisfies $\mu(\emptyset) = 0$ and countable additivity: $\mu(\bigsqcup A_n) = \sum \mu(A_n)$ for pairwise disjoint sets $A_n$.
