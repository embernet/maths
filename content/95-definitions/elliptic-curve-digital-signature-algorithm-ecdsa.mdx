## Definition

**Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA)**, used in Bitcoin, TLS 1.3, and most modern systems, replaces RSA's multiplicative group modulo $n$ with the group of points on an elliptic curve over a finite field. An elliptic curve over $\mathbb{F}_p$ is the set $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb{F}_p^2 : y^2 = x^3 + ax + b\} \cup \{\mathcal{O}\}$ with a group law defined by the chord-and-tangent construction. The discrete logarithm problem on such curves is believed harder than integer factorisation, allowing much smaller key sizes (256-bit ECDSA provides roughly the same security as 3072-bit RSA).
