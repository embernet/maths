## Definition

1. **Randomisation:** Allocate treatments randomly to experimental units. This controls for unknown confounding variables by distributing them evenly across groups (on average). It is also the basis for the probability calculations in ANOVA — without randomisation, the $F$-test has no valid interpretation.
