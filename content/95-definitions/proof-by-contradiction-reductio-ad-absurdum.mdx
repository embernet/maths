## Definition

**Proof by contradiction (reductio ad absurdum):** To prove $P$, assume $\neg P$ (the negation of $P$) and derive a logical contradiction — a statement $Q \wedge \neg Q$. Since a contradiction cannot be true, the assumption $\neg P$ must have been false, so $P$ must be true.
