## Definition

**Sequential testing** handles the streaming nature: a **CUSUM** (cumulative sum) chart monitors the cumulative log-likelihood ratio and raises an alarm when the cumulative sum exceeds a threshold. CUSUM controls the average run length (ARL) — the expected number of observations before a false alarm — at a desired level while minimising the delay to detection when an anomaly occurs.
