## Definition

**Ordinals** measure the order-type of a well-ordered set. After the finite ordinals $0, 1, 2, \ldots$ comes $\omega$ (the order-type of $\mathbb{N}$), then $\omega + 1$, $\omega \cdot 2$, $\omega^2$, $\omega^\omega$, $\varepsilon_0$, and beyond.
