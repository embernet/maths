## Definition

Their collaboration on **partitions** — the number of ways of writing an integer as a sum of positive integers — produced the **Hardy–Ramanujan asymptotic formula**: $p(n) \sim \frac{1}{4n\sqrt{3}} e^{\pi\sqrt{2n/3}}$ as $n \to \infty$. The proof used the **circle method**, a technique of complex analysis that became one of the central tools of analytic number theory. The formula is remarkable for its precision: $p(200) = 3{,}972{,}999{,}029{,}388$, and the formula gives this to within one integer.
