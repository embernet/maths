## Definition

His 1900 address to the International Congress of Mathematicians in Paris presented **23 problems** for the coming century: a manifesto and agenda for mathematics. They included the Riemann hypothesis ([Problem 8](/mathematicians/riemann)), the continuum hypothesis ([Problem 1](/mathematicians/cantor)), the consistency of arithmetic (Problem 2), and questions in algebraic geometry, number theory, physics, and the calculus of variations. Solving a Hilbert problem remains among the highest distinctions in mathematics.
