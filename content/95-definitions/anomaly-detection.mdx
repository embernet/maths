## Definition

Every large organisation runs **anomaly detection** systems on its network traffic and user activity: flagging behaviour that deviates significantly from established baselines. The same problem arises in banking (detecting fraudulent transactions), cloud infrastructure (detecting compromised accounts), and industrial control systems (detecting sensor anomalies). The mathematics is statistical hypothesis testing applied continuously to streaming data.
