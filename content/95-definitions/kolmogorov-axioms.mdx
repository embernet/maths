## Definition

The measure $P$ must satisfy the **Kolmogorov axioms**:
1. $P(A) \ge 0$ for all events $A$.
2. $P(\Omega) = 1$.
3. For pairwise disjoint events $A_1, A_2, \ldots$: $P\!\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^\infty A_i\right) = \sum_{i=1}^\infty P(A_i)$.
