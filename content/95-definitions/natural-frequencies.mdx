## Definition

**Natural frequencies** make this more intuitive: in 100,000 tested individuals, 100 have the disease and 99 test positive; 99,900 do not have the disease and 999 of these test positive. Of the $99 + 999 = 1098$ total positives, only 99 are genuine — about 9%.
