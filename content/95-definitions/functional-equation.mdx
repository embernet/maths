## Definition

[Riemann](/mathematicians/riemann) showed in 1859 that $\zeta(s)$ extends to a meromorphic function on all of $\mathbb{C}$ with a single simple pole at $s = 1$, and satisfies a **functional equation** relating $\zeta(s)$ to $\zeta(1-s)$.
