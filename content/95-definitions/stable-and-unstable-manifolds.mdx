## Definition

[Poincaré](/mathematicians/poincare) showed in 1890 that generic orbits of three gravitating bodies do not follow quasi-periodic paths but can exhibit wild, chaotic behaviour: trajectories that are highly sensitive to initial conditions. He introduced the **Poincaré section** (cross-section of a flow), **stable and unstable manifolds**, and the **homoclinic tangle** — the tangled intersection of stable and unstable manifolds that generates chaos.
