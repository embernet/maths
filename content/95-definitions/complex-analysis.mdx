## Definition

**Complex Analysis:** Needham's *Visual Complex Analysis* uses geometric intuition to make the subject feel as concrete as possible before it becomes abstract. Stein and Shakarchi's *Complex Analysis* (Princeton Lectures in Analysis, Vol. 2) is more rigorous and builds to the Riemann zeta function.
