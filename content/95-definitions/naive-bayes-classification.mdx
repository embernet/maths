## Definition

Approximately 45% of global email traffic is spam — 162 billion messages per day. Every email service runs a spam classifier to keep most of it out of your inbox. The simplest and most instructive implementation is **naïve Bayes classification**: a direct application of Bayes' theorem that works surprisingly well despite its simplifying assumption.
