## Definition

**Linear Algebra:** Sheldon Axler's *Linear Algebra Done Right* deliberately avoids determinants until late, organising the subject around linear maps and eigenvalues in a way that many students find more illuminating than the determinant-first approach. Gilbert Strang's *Introduction to Linear Algebra* is computational and geometric; his lecture videos on MIT OCW are excellent.
