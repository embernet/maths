## Definition

- **Paul's Online Math Notes** — [tutorial.math.lamar.edu](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu). Comprehensive, clearly written worked examples for calculus, differential equations, and linear algebra. Useful when you need to see a technique in action before attempting it yourself.
