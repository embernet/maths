## Definition

**MRI** measures the nuclear magnetic resonance of hydrogen protons. The raw measurements from an MRI scanner are samples of the **k-space** of the image — which is precisely the Fourier domain. Image reconstruction is then an inverse Fourier transform, with substantial signal processing to correct for hardware imperfections and motion artefacts.
