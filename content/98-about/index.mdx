## What this is

A free, standalone companion to mathematics — for anyone meeting these ideas, whether at work, as a hobby, in formal study, or out of plain curiosity. It assumes only school-level mathematics as a starting point and builds from there. Topics are arranged along the arc of a typical undergraduate curriculum — not because the site is only for university students, but because that progression (foundations → core → specialist → advanced) is a well-tested way to see how the whole subject fits together. Each topic has a short explanation, a real-world applications section, and a quiz, and the Theorems, Mathematicians, History and Applications sections cross-link to every topic, so you can follow whichever thread you find interesting.

The site is a static build: nothing runs on a server, no data is collected about you, and everything works offline once visited.
