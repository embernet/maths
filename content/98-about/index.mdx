## What this is

A standalone reference companion for any student about to start a UK-style undergraduate mathematics degree, written for a reader who has just finished A-Level Mathematics and Further Mathematics. Topics are introduced in the order a typical degree introduces them; each topic has a short professor-voice explanation, a real-world applications section, and a quiz. The Theorems, Mathematicians, History and Real-world applications sections cross-link to every topic, so you can travel through the curriculum by following whichever thread you find interesting.

The site is a static build: nothing runs on a server, no data is collected about you, and everything works offline once visited.
