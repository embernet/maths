        $$
F_n = \frac{\varphi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}, \qquad \varphi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}, \quad \psi = \frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}.
$$

The Fibonacci sequence — $1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, \dots$ — has an explicit non-recursive formula.

## History

Jacques Binet published it in 1843, but Abraham de Moivre had derived an equivalent expression in 1718 and Euler later. The formula is the eigenvalue decomposition of the linear recurrence $F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$.

## Why it matters

It is the prototype example of using linear algebra to solve a recurrence. The characteristic polynomial $x^2 = x + 1$ has roots $\varphi$ and $\psi$, and the general solution is a linear combination of $\varphi^n$ and $\psi^n$. This is exactly how spectral methods solve linear ODEs and difference equations.

## Where you'll meet it

In any second-year discrete-mathematics course as the worked example.
