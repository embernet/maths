## What it says

[Euler](/mathematicians/euler) published over 850 works, many of them book-length, spanning:
- Analysis: calculus, infinite series, differential equations, complex functions
- Number theory: Fermat's little theorem, quadratic reciprocity (stated), zeta function
- Algebra: fundamental theorem of algebra, complex numbers
- Geometry: graph theory, polyhedral formula $V - E + F = 2$
- Mechanics and physics: fluid dynamics, optics, celestial mechanics
- Music theory, ship design, Latin squares

He continued publishing after going completely blind in 1771, dictating to assistants. The St Petersburg Academy's printing backlog from his manuscripts lasted 47 years after his death.

## History

Euler spent most of his career at the St Petersburg Academy of Sciences and the Berlin Academy. He worked in a period when mathematics, physics, and astronomy were not yet fully separated disciplines, and his contributions range across all of them. His collected works (*Opera Omnia*) fill over 80 volumes.

## Why it matters

Euler shaped the landscape of eighteenth-century mathematics more than any other individual. Almost every area of mathematics has an "Euler theorem" or "Euler formula." His textbooks standardised notation and exposition. Students encounter Euler's work from Year 1 and continue to do so throughout their degrees.

## Real-world applications

Across all applications — Euler's methods, formulas, and frameworks appear in virtually every quantitative field.
